      Theoretical estimate notice

      Totals are based on entered ideal geometry and reference or custom density. Actual supplied mass may vary because of dimensional tolerances, radii, tapers, coatings, holes, cut-outs, moisture, composition, and manufacturing variation.

      Methodology

      See how the number is built.

      This workbench keeps mass, gross-section geometry, and optional elastic screening traceable. It explains where a result is exact for the idealized model, where it is approximate, and what must be verified.

      Core equation

      m=V×ρ

      Theoretical mass in kilograms
      V
      Net material volume in cubic metres

      Result status

      Reference, not certification

      A grade or product standard may identify composition, strength, dimensions, or tolerances without prescribing one universal density. That is why every material is labelled as a reference value, and custom supplier density is supported.

      • Use reference density for early design and quoting.
      • Use supplier or measured density for purchasing-critical work.
      • Use the active manufacturer or standard catalog kg/m for rolled sections when available.

      Structural sections

      Manual models are idealized

      Manual I, H, channel, angle, hollow, and Z models use clean geometric rectangles. Standard rolled products include radii, slopes, tolerances, and tabulated nominal mass. The UI marks that distinction instead of calling an approximation exact.

      Section geometry

      Area, stiffness, bending & buckling geometry

      The calculator reports centroidal A, Ix, Iy, Ixy, principal inertias, elastic section moduli for both extreme fibres, and radii of gyration. It uses S = I / c and r = √(I / A). Unsymmetric sections retain coupled-axis behavior instead of assuming the entered axes are principal.

      Torsion

      Jp and Jt are deliberately separate

      The polar area moment Jp = Ix + Iy is reported as geometry. Saint-Venant Jt governs free-warping elastic twist θ = TL / (GJt) and is calculated only by a shape-appropriate exact or stated thin-wall model. Jp is never substituted for Jt except where they are equal for circular sections.

      Optional demand screen

      Simple elastic checks, with exclusions visible

      User-entered compression and biaxial moment produce a gross-section normal-stress envelope. Sourced E enables Euler flexural and four elementary beam-deflection cases; E and Poisson ratio enable free-warping twist where Jt is supported. These results exclude code resistance factors, load combinations, local and lateral-torsional buckling, connections, fatigue, shear interaction, imperfections, and project acceptance limits.

      Mechanical evidence

      Properties are entered, never guessed from density

      Yield or proof strength, UTS, E, Poisson ratio, elongation, and hardness with its scale are optional user/supplier evidence. Record product form, condition or temper, temperature, direction, specimen basis, and source. UTS, hardness, and elongation are shown for selection context; they are not converted into an automatic allowable.

      Units

      One SI calculation core

      Metric and imperial inputs are converted to metres before any geometry formula runs. Mass is stored in kilograms and displayed as t, kg, g, mg, or µg without changing the underlying value.

      Data quality

      Why legacy citations were changed

      The original dataset attached some standards to materials even when those standards described unrelated products or test methods. The upgraded tool removes those misleading claims, retains the useful indicative density value, and tells the user to verify it from an appropriate supplier or technical source.
