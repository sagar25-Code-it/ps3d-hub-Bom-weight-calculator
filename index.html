    Theoretical estimate notice

    Totals are based on entered ideal geometry and reference or custom density. Actual supplied mass may vary because of dimensional tolerances, radii, tapers, coatings, holes, cut-outs, moisture, composition, and manufacturing variation.

    Methodology

    See how the number is built.

    This workbench keeps the simple relationship visible: material volume × density = theoretical mass. It also explains where a reference is strong, where it is only indicative, and what must be verified.

    Core equation

    m=V×ρ
    m
    Theoretical mass in kilograms
    V
    Net material volume in cubic metres
    ρ
    Density in kilograms per cubic metre

    Result status

    Reference, not certification

    A grade or product standard may identify composition, strength, dimensions, or tolerances without prescribing one universal density. That is why every material is labelled as a reference value, and custom supplier density is supported.

    • Use reference density for early design and quoting.
    • Use supplier or measured density for purchasing-critical work.
    • Use the active manufacturer or standard catalog kg/m for rolled sections when available.

    Structural sections

    Manual models are idealized

    Manual I, H, channel, angle, hollow, and Z models use clean geometric rectangles. Standard rolled products include radii, slopes, tolerances, and tabulated nominal mass. The UI marks that distinction instead of calling an approximation exact.

    Units

    One SI calculation core

    Metric and imperial inputs are converted to metres before any geometry formula runs. Mass is stored in kilograms and displayed as t, kg, g, mg, or µg without changing the underlying value.

    Data quality

    Why legacy citations were changed

    The original dataset attached some standards to materials even when those standards described unrelated products or test methods. The upgraded tool removes those misleading claims, retains the useful indicative density value, and tells the user to verify it from an appropriate supplier or technical source.
